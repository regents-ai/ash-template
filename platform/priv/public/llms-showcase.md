## Build skills and working examples

This site is also the public demo of the Ash Template starter kit, the one Regent's sites are built from.

- [Agent guide to the build skills]({{origin}}/build/skill.md): how to fetch them and check each download.
- [Build skills index]({{origin}}/.well-known/agent-skills/index.json) in the Agent Skills Discovery v0.2.0 format, and [the same skills for people]({{origin}}/skills).
- [Showcase]({{origin}}/showcase): every shared component, in light and dark. Its [catalog]({{origin}}/showcase/catalog) lists them as JSON.
- [Sign in with Privy]({{origin}}/showcase/privy): sign-in, working.
- [Wallet buttons]({{origin}}/showcase/wallet) on Base Sepolia, a test network. Only a signed-in person can press them, from their own wallet, and each press needs a little Base Sepolia test ETH for the network fee.
- [Pay with USDC]({{origin}}/showcase/payments): how paying looks on every Regent site, with sample figures and paying switched off.
- [Your funds]({{origin}}/showcase/funds): how money looks on every Regent site: what's in your wallet, what you've committed, adding funds with a card, sending and cashing out, with sample figures and every money button switched off.
- [Discussion]({{origin}}/showcase/discussion): how a question and its replies read on a Regent site, with sample posts; likes are not kept.
- [Live figures]({{origin}}/showcase/figures): a figure that is the same for everyone, read once and shown on every open page: Base's newest block.
- [Grammar]({{origin}}/showcase/grammar): the pieces every Regent page is made of, by name; [how they move]({{origin}}/showcase/grammar/verbs); and [the six layers]({{origin}}/showcase/grammar/layers) from the ground to a tip, each showing its height by its edge, its fill and how it arrives.
- [Motion lab]({{origin}}/animations): every motion the kit uses.
